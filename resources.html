SSL/TLS Certificate fundamentals

Quick Guide to SSL/TLS

SSL stands for Secure Socket Layer. It’s a technology that establishes a secure connection between a visitor’s web browser and your website so that all communication transmitted between them is encrypted and, therefore, secure. SSL is also used for transmitting secure email, secure files, and other forms of information.
